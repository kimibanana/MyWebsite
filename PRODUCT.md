## Platform

web

## Users

The buyer is whoever runs marketing and the website for a healthcare or medical business. That can be the doctor or owner doing it after hours, or an in-house marketing person or team at a larger clinic, group or hospital. They are busy, often burned by an agency before, and judge an agency on whether its work is clear, accountable and safe under healthcare advertising rules.

The audience is global first. Clinics in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Singapore, UAE and New Zealand come before the home market, the Philippines, which is still served. Prices are shown in USD with a local currency switcher.

## Product Purpose

Web Frontier is a marketing agency that specializes in healthcare and medical businesses. It brings clinics more of the right patients through medical SEO and local SEO (Google Business Profile, Map Pack), healthcare website design, Google Ads, Local Services Ads and website care.

The website exists to turn a visiting clinic decision-maker into a lead: a free growth audit request, a booked call, a form submission or a WhatsApp message. Success is qualified healthcare leads, not traffic.

## Positioning

Healthcare and medical specialization combined with fixed-price 30-day sprints: each sprint has a written scope, a set price and a delivery report, with no long-term contract and no open-ended retainer. The client judges progress sprint by sprint. General web and SEO agencies that sell to every industry on retainers cannot truthfully claim this.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static Astro site deployed on Netlify; content lives in `src/data/site.ts`, `src/data/services.ts`, `src/data/content.ts` and `src/content/resources/`. Keep it static, fast and nearly JavaScript-free.
- Sprint catalogue: Foundation Sprint, Local, Content, Authority and Ads sprints, bundles (Local Starter, Local Growth, Market Leader), website sprints (Launch, Growth Site) and Care plans. Prices are suggested starting points and not yet final.
- The sprint promise is confirmed: if any item in a sprint's written scope is not delivered within 30 days, work continues at no extra cost until it is done.
- Healthcare marketing must respect advertising and health-claim policies and patient privacy. No guaranteed rankings or results.
- Still open: custom domain, booking link, founding year, public address, social accounts (not live yet), final local prices, privacy policy legal review.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: founder's 10+ years in SEO, web and paid search; the confirmed sprint promise; 12 published guides in `src/content/resources/`.
- Missing: there are no real client results, case studies, testimonials, logos, ratings or stats yet. Current case studies, testimonials and the hero dashboard are samples labeled as such, and `site.stats` stays hidden until real numbers exist. Future work must not invent or present proof as real.

## Product Principles

1. Clarity earns trust: show scope, price and deliverables plainly, because the buyer has been burned by vague agencies.
2. Healthcare first: every claim and pattern should fit clinics, their patients and their advertising rules.
3. Respect the buyer's time: a busy doctor or marketing lead should understand the offer and the next step in seconds.
4. Honest proof only: leave a proof slot empty rather than fill it with something fabricated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site already ships a skip link, semantic headings, accessible forms and reduced-motion support; keep these. Patients' families and older practice owners are part of the audience, so readable text and clear contrast matter.
