For most clinics, the Google Business Profile (GBP) brings more new-patient calls than the website does. It’s what appears in Google Maps and the local pack, and AI tools refer to it often. Yet many clinics set it up once and never touch it again.

Use this checklist to make yours work as hard as possible.

## 1. Get the basics exactly right

- [ ] **Business name** exactly as it appears on your signage, with no added keywords
- [ ] **Address** matching your website and other listings, character for character
- [ ] **Phone number** that reaches your front desk, local format
- [ ] **Website link** to the matching location page, with UTM tags so you can track visits in analytics
- [ ] **Hours**, including holiday hours, kept up to date
- [ ] **Verified** ownership, with at least two staff members as managers

## 2. Choose categories carefully

Your **primary category** is one of the strongest ranking signals you control.

- Choose the most specific category that fits, such as “Cosmetic dentist”, “Dermatologist” or “Fertility clinic”, rather than a generic “Medical clinic”.
- Add relevant **secondary categories** for other major services.
- Check what categories top-ranking competitors use (free browser extensions can show this).

## 3. List every service

- [ ] Add each treatment you offer as a **service**, with a short description
- [ ] Include price ranges where appropriate and allowed in your market
- [ ] Use the words patients search for, such as “teeth whitening”, not only clinical terms

## 4. Write a useful description

You have 750 characters. Use them to explain:

- Who you help and what you specialize in
- Your doctors and their experience
- What makes the clinic different: technology, languages spoken, HMOs accepted, parking

Avoid keyword stuffing, links and promotional claims you can’t support.

## 5. Add real photos and video

- [ ] Exterior shots so patients can find you, including the building and entrance
- [ ] Reception, treatment rooms and equipment
- [ ] Doctors and team, with consent
- [ ] Short video tours
- [ ] Fresh photos added every month

Real photos consistently outperform stock images in engagement.

## 6. Fill in attributes and booking

- [ ] Accessibility, parking, languages, payment methods and other relevant attributes
- [ ] **Booking link** to your online booking or appointment page
- [ ] **Messaging** enabled only if someone will reply quickly

## 7. Build a steady flow of reviews

Reviews are one of the biggest factors in Maps rankings and in patient choice.

- Ask every satisfied patient, ideally the same day, with a direct review link by WhatsApp, SMS or email.
- Aim for a steady flow each week rather than occasional bursts.
- Never offer incentives for reviews or post reviews on patients’ behalf.
- Reply to every review within a few days, **without confirming they’re a patient or mentioning any health details**.

## 8. Answer questions and post updates

- [ ] Seed the Q&A section with your most common questions and clear answers
- [ ] Post weekly updates: new services, seasonal check-ups, doctor introductions, clinic news
- [ ] Monitor for public edits and suggested changes from other users

## 9. Keep it consistent everywhere

Your GBP should match your website, Facebook page, directories and medical association listings. Inconsistencies hurt both Maps rankings and AI recommendations.

## 10. Measure what matters

- Calls, direction requests and website clicks in GBP insights
- Maps grid rankings for your top treatments
- Booked appointments from GBP traffic, tracked with UTM tags

## Common reasons clinic profiles get suspended

- Keywords added to the business name
- Virtual office or incorrect address
- Duplicate profiles for the same location
- Sudden major changes to name, address and categories at once

If your profile is suspended, fix the issue first, then request reinstatement with documents such as permits and photos of signage.
