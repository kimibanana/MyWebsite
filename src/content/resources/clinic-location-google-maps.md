Google decides which clinics appear in the Maps “local pack” using three broad factors: **relevance** (do you offer what was searched?), **prominence** (how well-known and well-reviewed are you?) and **proximity** (how close are you to the person searching?).

You can improve relevance and prominence with good marketing. Proximity is mostly fixed the day you sign your lease, which makes location one of the biggest marketing decisions a clinic makes.

## Why proximity matters so much

When a patient searches “pediatrician near me” or even just “pediatrician”, Google assumes they want someone nearby. A clinic with fewer reviews that sits a few blocks away will often outrank a better-reviewed clinic across town.

In dense cities like Metro Manila, Singapore or central London, your strong Maps visibility might cover only a few kilometers. In suburbs and provincial cities with fewer competitors, it can stretch much further.

## Measuring your real reach with a grid scan

Checking rankings from your own desk only tells you how you rank at your clinic. A **grid scan** checks your Maps position from dozens of points across your area, for example a 7 × 7 grid. It shows:

- Where you’re in the top three
- Where you drop off
- Which competitors win the areas you’re missing

We run grid scans for each priority treatment, because a clinic can dominate “dentist” nearby but barely appear for “dental implants” a few kilometers away.

## Choosing a location with Maps in mind

If you’re opening a new clinic or branch, look at local search before you sign:

1. **Search your main treatments from the candidate address.** Who appears in the top three? How many reviews do they have?
2. **Check competitor density.** Ten established clinics on the same street means a long climb.
3. **Look at where patients come from.** Near large residential areas, offices, schools or transport hubs is usually better than an isolated spot.
4. **Consider visibility and access.** Mall and medical-building locations bring foot traffic and are easy for patients to find, but may sit near many competitors.
5. **Think about your service area.** If you want patients from two cities, a location between them may serve both better than one in the center of either.

## Winning beyond your immediate area

Proximity limits Maps, but it doesn’t limit all search. To reach patients further away:

- **Strengthen relevance.** Add every treatment you offer to your Google Business Profile, choose accurate primary and secondary categories, and write a detailed description.
- **Build prominence.** A steady flow of recent, detailed reviews and local mentions can extend your reach into more competitive areas.
- **Rank organically.** Treatment pages can rank in regular results for patients outside your Maps radius, especially for specialized treatments people travel for.
- **Use ads** to appear at the top for specific areas where Maps won’t reach.
- **Open real branches** where demand justifies it, each with its own Google Business Profile and location page.

## What not to do

- **Virtual offices or fake addresses.** They break Google’s guidelines and can get your profile suspended.
- **Keyword-stuffed business names** like “Smile Clinic Best Dentist Makati”. They can be reported and lead to suspension.
- **Duplicate profiles** for the same location.
- **Hiding the address** on a clinic patients visit in person.

- [ ] Google Business Profile verified, with accurate categories, hours, services and photos

- [ ] Clinic schema with the correct address and coordinates
- [ ] A steady review request process
- [ ] Consistent listings on Facebook and key healthcare directories
