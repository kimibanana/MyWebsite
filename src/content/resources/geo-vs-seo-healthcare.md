Agencies now sell “GEO” as if it were a separate discipline from SEO. For clinics, the truth is simpler: **they share most of the same foundations**, and the differences are mostly about emphasis.

## Quick definitions

- **SEO (search engine optimization):** making your clinic rank in Google and other search engines, including Google Maps.
- **GEO (generative engine optimization):** making your clinic cited and recommended in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google’s AI Overviews.

## Where they overlap

| Work | Helps SEO | Helps GEO |
|---|---|---|
| Complete, accurate Google Business Profile | ✓ | ✓ |
| Consistent clinic details across the web | ✓ | ✓ |
| Strong treatment and doctor pages | ✓ | ✓ |
| Reviews | ✓ | ✓ |
| Schema markup | ✓ | ✓ |
| Fast, accessible, secure website | ✓ | ✓ |
| Mentions on trusted sites | ✓ | ✓ |

If you do these well, you’re already doing most of GEO.

## Where they differ

### Content style

SEO has historically focused on matching keywords. GEO rewards content that **answers complete questions directly**, so it can be quoted. Write clear opening answers, use FAQs and structure information in lists and tables.

### Breadth of sources

Google Maps rankings lean heavily on your Google Business Profile and its reviews. AI tools draw from **a wider range of sources**: other review sites, directories, forums, news and video. Your reputation needs to be visible beyond Google.

### Freshness

AI answers tend to favor recently updated information. Regularly reviewing and dating your medical content matters even more for GEO.

### Measurement

SEO has mature tools: rankings, Search Console, Maps grids. GEO measurement is still developing, so it relies on AI referral traffic, regular prompt checks and asking patients how they found you.

## How to split your effort

For most clinics today, we recommend roughly:

- **70% shared foundations:** Google Business Profile, treatment pages, reviews, technical health, schema
- **20% SEO-specific:** Maps grid optimization, local links, keyword targeting
- **10% GEO-specific:** answer-first formatting, review diversity, prompt monitoring, broader mentions

As AI search grows in your market, shift more effort toward GEO-specific work.

## The bottom line

It’s not GEO *or* SEO. It’s one well-run search program that makes your clinic easy to find, easy to trust and easy to recommend, whether the patient asks Google or an AI assistant.
