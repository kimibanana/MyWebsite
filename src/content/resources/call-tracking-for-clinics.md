Ask most clinics where their patients come from and you’ll hear “Google” or “Facebook”. Ask which campaign, keyword or page and the answer is usually a shrug. That’s because most bookings start with a **phone call or chat**, and most clinics don’t track them.

Without that data, your ads are optimized for the wrong thing and your marketing budget is a guess.

## Why calls and chats matter so much in healthcare

Patients often want to talk to a person before booking, especially for higher-value treatments. They have questions about cost, pain, availability or HMO coverage. In the Philippines and many other markets, WhatsApp, Viber and Messenger are just as important as phone calls.

If you only track web forms, you might be missing most of your leads.

## How call tracking works

1. **Unique tracking numbers** are assigned to different sources: Google Ads, Facebook, your website and print.
2. **Dynamic number insertion** swaps the number shown on your website based on how the visitor arrived.
3. Calls forward to your clinic as normal. Patients don’t notice anything different.
4. The system records **which source, campaign and keyword** produced each call, plus duration and outcome.

Google Ads also offers its own call tracking for calls from ads and from your website.

## What to track

- **Qualified calls.** Calls lasting more than 60–90 seconds, which filters out wrong numbers and quick hang-ups.
- **First-time callers** versus existing patients.
- **WhatsApp and Messenger clicks** from your site and ads.
- **Booked appointments** and, ideally, attended appointments.

## Closing the loop: from call to booked patient

Tracking calls is step one. The real power comes from telling your ad platforms which calls became patients:

1. Your front desk marks each new enquiry as **booked**, **not booked** or **not a patient** in a simple CRM or spreadsheet.
2. That outcome is matched to the call or click.
3. Outcomes are imported back to Google Ads or Meta as offline conversions.
4. The platforms learn to find more people like your actual patients.

Even a basic version of this process often improves cost per patient within a few months.

## Protecting patient privacy

Healthcare tracking needs extra care:

- **Never send health information** such as diagnosis, treatment or symptoms to ad platforms. Send only that a conversion happened and its value.
- **Be careful with call recordings.** Inform callers where required, restrict access and set retention limits.
- **Follow local law,** such as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 in the Philippines, HIPAA in the US or UK GDPR, and use vendors that support compliance where needed.
- **Keep forms non-clinical.** Ask what treatment someone is interested in, not their medical history.

## Keeping local SEO safe

Call tracking can confuse your local listings if done carelessly:

- Keep your **real clinic number** as the main number on Google Business Profile and directories.
- Use a tracking number only as an additional number on your Google profile if needed.
- Use **dynamic insertion** on your website so search engines still see your real number in the page’s code and schema.

## Setup checklist

- [ ] Choose a call tracking provider that works in your country
- [ ] Set up dynamic number insertion on your website
- [ ] Track calls over 60–90 seconds as conversions
- [ ] Track WhatsApp, Viber and Messenger clicks
- [ ] Create a simple booked/not-booked process for your front desk
- [ ] Import outcomes into your ad platforms monthly or automatically
- [ ] Review privacy settings and call recording notices
