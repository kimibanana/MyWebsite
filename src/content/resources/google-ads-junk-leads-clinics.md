You’re paying for clicks and the phone is ringing, but your front desk keeps saying the same thing: “These aren’t real patients.” Wrong numbers, job applicants, people asking for free advice, patients hundreds of kilometers away.

Junk leads are one of the most common complaints we hear from clinics running Google Ads. The good news is that they usually have fixable causes.

## Cause 1: Your keywords are too broad

Broad keywords like “dentist” or “skin care” match all sorts of searches, including “dentist salary”, “dentist school” and “skin care products”.

**Fix:**

- Use phrase and exact match for your core treatment keywords.
- Build a strong **negative keyword list**: jobs, salary, course, school, free, DIY, home remedy, government, and competitors you don’t want to target.
- Review the **search terms report** weekly and add new negatives.

## Cause 2: Your location settings are wrong

By default, Google may show your ads to people “interested in” your area, not just people in it.

**Fix:**

- Set location targeting to **“Presence: people in or regularly in your targeted locations.”**
- Target a realistic radius around each clinic.
- Exclude areas you don’t serve.

## Cause 3: You’re optimizing for the wrong conversion

If Google is told that every form submission or click-to-call is a success, it will find the cheapest ones, which are often the lowest quality.

**Fix:**

- Track calls longer than 60–90 seconds, not every call.
- Track completed bookings where your booking system allows.
- Feed back which leads became patients (see below).

## Cause 4: Your forms are too easy

A one-field form attracts spam and low-commitment enquiries.

**Fix:**

- Ask for name, phone and the treatment they’re interested in.
- Add a simple qualifying question, such as preferred clinic branch or appointment timing.
- Use spam protection.
- Never ask for detailed medical information in an ad form.

## Cause 5: Ads and landing pages are unclear

If your ad says “Affordable Dental Care” and sends people to your homepage, you’ll attract bargain hunters and confused visitors.

**Fix:**

- Mention the specific treatment, location and a price signal or “from” price where your market’s rules allow.
- Send each ad group to a dedicated treatment landing page.
- Use ad assets for location, call and structured snippets.

## Cause 6: Nobody follows up fast enough

Sometimes leads look like junk because the clinic called back two days later. Patients often contact several clinics and book with whoever responds first.

**Fix:**

- Respond to every web lead within minutes during clinic hours.
- Use WhatsApp, SMS or instant replies to acknowledge out-of-hours enquiries.
- Track response times and outcomes.

## The most powerful fix: tell Google which leads became patients

Google’s bidding gets dramatically better when it knows which clicks turned into booked patients, not just leads. You can do this by:

1. Capturing the click ID (GCLID) with each lead
2. Marking leads as **booked** or **attended** in your CRM or spreadsheet
3. Importing those outcomes back into Google Ads, manually or automatically

Over a few weeks, Google learns what a real patient looks like and finds more of them.

**Privacy note:** only send minimal, non-clinical data back to ad platforms. Never share diagnoses, treatments received or other health information.

## Quick checklist

- [ ] Phrase/exact match keywords with a strong negative list
- [ ] “Presence” location targeting
- [ ] Conversions = qualified calls and bookings, not every click
- [ ] Qualifying forms with spam protection
- [ ] Treatment-specific landing pages
- [ ] Fast follow-up process
- [ ] Offline conversion import for booked patients
